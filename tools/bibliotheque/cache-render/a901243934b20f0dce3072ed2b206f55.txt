A Natural Stage Companion
Built on decades of vocal microphone expertise, the e 935 offers a balanced combination of focus and forgiveness for artists who demand reliable, natural vocal reproduction. The cardioid pickup pattern stays locked onto your voice even when your mic technique isn’t perfect, helping you sound confident and clear whether you’re moving freely or facing loud stage monitors.
Hear Your Voice Gain Intensity and Size
From rock and pop to folk and soul, the e 935 adapts effortlessly to your vocal style. Transparent high-end frequencies add brilliance and lift, while warm lower mids bring body and richness. A precise boost in the 2.5 to 3 kHz range gives your vocals the presence to stand out. This targeted midrange tuning adds weight and helps singers project to be more “in the mix”—even without heavy compression or EQ. Pick up an e 935, and you’ll hear your voice instantly gain intensity and size.
Sing Longer, Perform Stronger
Thanks to an industry-leading +6 dB gain before feedback, the e 935 lets you sing with less strain compared to other leading microphones. You won’t have to push your voice as hard to cut through the mix, helping you perform longer sets, recover faster, and take on more gigs. It’s not just better sound—it’s a microphone that supports your voice, your stamina, and your career.
Engineered for the Real World
The e 935 is built for life on the road. With a rugged metal housing, shock-mounted capsule, hum-compensating coil, and sealed internal air chamber, it minimizes handling noise and shrugs off the challenges of touring. Assembled in Germany and backed by Sennheiser’s 2-year (USA: 10-year) warranty, the e 935 vocal microphone delivers consistent performance night after night, year after year.
Ready for Any Stage
Ideal for lead and backup singers, singer-songwriters, worship teams, and working musicians of every genre, the e 935 offers the flexibility and durability needed for consistent, high-quality performances anywhere you play.
