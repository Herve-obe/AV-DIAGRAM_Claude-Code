ND96

Supercardioid Dynamic Vocal Microphone

Where to buy
Documentation & Downloads

Push the vocal louder before it rings. The ND96's structural and acoustic design lifts gain before feedback well past most mics, so the lead voice cuts through a loud stage and stays there. A selectable vocal tuning trims low-mids and lifts upper-midrange presence for intelligibility in high-energy reinforcement. A large Mylar diaphragm tracks fast transients across a 30 Hz to 15 kHz close response, and a four-point shockmount cuts handling noise. Hard-rock stage, festival, loud worship: the vocal wins.

Key features
Win the loud stage
Engineered for significantly higher gain before feedback.
Dial in the presence
Selectable vocal tuning trims low-mids, lifts upper-mid clarity.
Cut through the mix
Supercardioid pattern for present, intelligible vocals.
Reach the full range
Close response from 30 Hz to 15 kHz.
Lose the handling noise
Four-point shockmount and humbucking coil built in.
Rich tone, fast attack
Large Mylar diaphragm pairs big-surface tonal balance with low-mass speed for exceptionally fast transients.
