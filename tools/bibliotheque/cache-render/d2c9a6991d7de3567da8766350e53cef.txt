15327 lumen RGB LED projection luminaire
Full 'moving head' functionality with 540° pan and 270° tilt
High power projector with motorized 8° to 43° zoom range for large outdoor applications
Total freedom to create stage and theatrical effects in the great outdoors
Unsurpassed large-scale multiple lighting effects
Built-in patented dehumidifier in both head and base
5-year warranty
IP66-rating and corrosion class C5-M (marine); ideal for coastal and offshore installations

The G-Spot POI is a unique, IP66-rated moving head and high power projection luminaire for permanent outdoor installations with all the functionalities, normally, only accessible for indoor applications. The G-Spot POI has full RGB color mixing and features variable speed, pulse effect, and strobe effects. All this makes it the perfect luminaire for high exposure areas, including coastal and offshore areas with high salinity and acid air such as cruise ships, pool areas, beach discos, and bars. SGM's G-Spot won the prestigious top industry PLASA Award for innovation in 2013 due to its revolutionary features.
