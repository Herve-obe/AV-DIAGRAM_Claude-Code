Ovation Rêve F-3 IP is an outdoor-rated motorized fresnel incorporating our full spectrum (RGBAM) Rêve light engine. With easy on-board control dials and quiet operation, Rêve F-3 IP represents a welcome solution for applications and markets in need of soft controllable washes. Ready to go anywhere, rain or shine, its innovative adjustable yoke also allows it to be mounted anywhere, even in low clearance areas. Featuring an optical system designed to answer the call for longer throws with an extended zoom range that can narrow from 60 degrees all the way down to 10 degrees in order to achieve impressive reach. A versatile lighting instrument at home in theaters, houses of worship, on TV and Film sets, as well as the rental and production world. Flawlessly smooth dimming, +/- green adjustment, linear CCT and adjustable Pulse Width Modulation ensure performance that looks as good on camera as it does live.
Features
Full Spectrum LED Fresnel

Provides high-quality fresnel lighting suitable for professional theatre, film, and production environments.

IP65 Weatherproof Rating

Fully rated for dust and water protection, enabling reliable use both indoors and outdoors.

On-Board Control

Features three dedicated control dials for direct, manual adjustment of multiple parameters.

Adjustable Color Temperature

Offers linear color temperature presets from 2800 K to 8000 K with high color rendering accuracy.

Virtual Color Wheel

Includes a digital color wheel with presets matched to popular professional gel colors.

Advanced Color Correction

Allows for plus/minus green adjustment and emulated red-shift via DMX or on-board controls.

Remote Device Management

Supports RDM for convenient remote addressing, configuration, and monitoring.

Flicker-Free Operation

Adjustable Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) ensures the light output is free of flickering on camera.

Silent Operation

Engineered for virtually silent performance, making it ideal for noise-sensitive applications.

18-Bit Dimming

Delivers ultra-smooth dimming curves and speeds to complement any lighting scheme.

Adjustable Yoke

An innovative yoke design allows for flexible mounting in low-clearance areas.

USB Software Port

A dedicated USB port allows for simple and convenient software updates.
