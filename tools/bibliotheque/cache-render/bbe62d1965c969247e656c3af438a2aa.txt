Dynamic Cardioid Microphone

For those ready to move beyond the stale standard of yesterday and embrace a fresh, modern sound. The e 835 is a lead and backup vocal stage mic engineered to perform under pressure, delivering consistent sound with a tight cardioid pattern that keeps your voice clear and up front — even when you move off-axis. A gentle presence boost adds natural projection and clarity, while minimal proximity effect keeps lows controlled at any distance. Built with precision German engineering and backed by an industry-leading 10-year warranty in the USA covering all parts and labor (2 years in all other regions), the e 835 is your workhorse built to perform.

The Workhorse of the Stage
Delivering the clarity and reliability live performers rely on, the e 835 is built for the stage with precision German engineering — and it’s priced for everyone. While some other mics might look the part, you’ll feel the difference with an e 835 in your hand. The weight, the refined details, and of course, the sound.  Whether it’s your first serious mic or your trusted companion on tour, the e 835 keeps professional performance within reach, night after night. 
Clear, Present, and Unmistakably Yours
The e 835 is voiced for definition and detail. It captures vocals with natural power that cuts through any mix. Its tight cardioid pattern focuses on your voice, rejecting unwanted noise from other sources. High output and a refined frequency response give every word more presence — crisp highs, solid mids, and a warmth that stays natural. Step in close or move off-axis — your tone stays consistent and open.
Made to Withstand the Road
Engineered to withstand the toughest conditions while continuing to deliver flawless sound, the e 835 features a rugged metal housing, hum-compensating coil, and advanced capsule suspension to protect against impact and handling noise. Feedback stays under control, even on loud stages. From clubs to open-air venues, the e 835 stays true, session after session, stage after stage.
Your Voice, Elevated
Confidence in every performance starts with trusting your sound. The e 835 is built for singers and musicians who want reliability, clarity, and freedom to simply perform. Discover why so many performers and engineers reach for the e 835. Power, clarity, and lasting performance — it’s everything a live mic should be.  
 
For many, the old standard is good enough. But for those with a passion for more, there’s the Sennheiser e 835. Experience the e 835 and hear the Sennheiser difference.
