WELL FIT is a must for event lighting. A discrete, high powered LED up-lighter, this small battery powered wash light comes in a reflective chrome housing designed to blend into any decor. WELL Fit can be controlled wirelessly either by W-DMX or by an included IR remote; alternatively, it can be controlled manually from the OLED display on the fixture. All of these features combined make the WELL Fit a perfect solution for quick setup and distinguished accent lighting.

Features
Wireless Battery-Powered Operation

A completely wireless, IP65-rated up-light featuring four high-powered, quad-color RGBA LEDs.

Versatile Control Options

Operate the fixture using wireless DMX, the included IR remote, or by recalling built-in automated programs.

Discreet Chrome Exterior

The polished chrome housing allows the fixture to blend seamlessly into its surroundings.

Charging Flight Case

Ships inside a 6-slot, drop-in road case for easy storage and simultaneous recharging of the batteries.

Extended Battery Performance

Get up to 8 hours of operation at full output from a single charge, with a quick 5-hour recharge time.

Secure Installation

Features an M12 threaded insert for simple clamp installation and a Kensington lock position to secure your investment.
