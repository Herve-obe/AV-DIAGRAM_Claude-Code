Dynamic Guitar Amp Microphone for Studio and Stage

The Sennheiser e 906 is a premium dynamic microphone designed for guitarists who demand authentic tone with the flexibility to fit any style. Engineered for fast transient response, high SPL handling, and rugged reliability, the e 906 transmits your true amp sound—direct, lively, and free from unwanted coloration.

Capture Every Style
Whether you're chasing warm jazz tones, classic rock crunch, or aggressive modern metal, the e 906 delivers. A three-stage presence switch adapts the mic’s tonal character to match your sound—boost presence for more attack and bite, keep it neutral for natural amp reproduction, or roll it off for smoother, darker tones. Bright mode boosts presence—ideal for aggressive metal or cutting leads. Flat mode delivers a true-to-amp response—perfect for classic rock. Dark mode rolls off the edge for warm jazz, blues, or vintage tones.
Fast, Focused, and Natural
The e 906’s extraordinarily fast transient response captures the true attack and personality of your playing. Every pick scrape, every power chord, every nuanced riff translates with directness and energy.
Designed for Real-World Stages
Inspired by the needs of working musicians, the e 906 guitar amp microphone features a flat form factor so the mic can hang straight in front of your speaker—no stand required. A hum-compensating coil, shock-mounted capsule, and rugged metal housing ensure professional performance even under the toughest demands.
 
Beyond guitars, the e 906 is also a secret weapon for snare drums and brass. Its fast attack, high SPL handling, and compact shape make it a go-to for hard-hitting sources on tight stages.
