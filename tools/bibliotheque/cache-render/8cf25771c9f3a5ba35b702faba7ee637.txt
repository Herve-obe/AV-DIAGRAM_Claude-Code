Ovation Rêve E-3

The Ovation Rêve E-3 is a high-output, multi-color LED ellipsoidal that fuses a full spectrum of saturated colors with a range of precisely tunable whites, complete with emulated red-shift and virtually silent operation.
Ideal for theatrical productions, broadcast studios, houses of worship, and any application requiring silent operation with both vibrant, full-color rendering and high-quality white light from a single fixture.

Ovation Rêve E-3 is the brightest, multi-color LED ellipsoidal that also has the capability of producing perfectly tunable whites. It’s a new kind of epic story-telling machine that fuses the best of both worlds into one totally reimagined LED light source. Color Temperature presets ranging from 2800K to 8000K maintain outstanding brightness and a high quality of light, with an emulated “red shift” for a tungsten feel. Ovation Rêve E-3 also features several dimming curves, a virtually silent operation with fan-off modes, and a series of technician-friendly features such as an innovative, adjustable yoke that makes mounting in low clearance situations a breeze. With Ovation Rêve E-3 you can seamlessly transition from captivating a wide range of saturated colors in class-leading output to distinct whites with +/- green adjustments using one fixture. No ifs, or buts. Simply and categorically unrivaLED.

Features
Full Spectrum LED ERS Fixture

Provides high-quality, ERS-style lighting suitable for professional theatre, film, and production environments.

Adjustable Color Temperature

Offers linear color temperature presets from 2800 K to 8000 K with high CRI and CQS for accurate color reproduction.

Virtual Color Wheel

Includes a virtual color wheel with colors precisely matched to popular theatrical gel swatches.

Precise Color Control

Fine-tune your output with plus/minus green adjustment and emulated red-shift, accessible via DMX or on-board controls.

Remote Device Management

RDM capability allows for remote addressing and configuration, adding flexibility to your setup.

Flicker-Free Operation

Adjustable Pulse Width Modulation ensures the light source is flicker-free, making it perfect for on-camera use.

Silent Operation

Engineered for virtually silent operation, this fixture is ideal for noise-sensitive studio, film, and theatre applications.

Ultra-Smooth 18-Bit Dimming

Achieve seamless fades and transitions with ultra-smooth 18-bit dimming curves and adjustable speeds.

Adjustable Yoke

An innovative adjustable yoke allows for easy mounting and positioning, even in areas with low clearance.

Integrated Gobo Rotator Control

Offers direct power, data, and control connections for the Ovation GR-1 gobo rotator accessory.

USB Software Updates

A convenient USB port allows for simple fixture software updates to keep the unit current.
