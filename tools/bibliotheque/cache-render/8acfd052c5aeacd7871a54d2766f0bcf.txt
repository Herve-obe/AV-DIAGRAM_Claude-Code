TVL2000 II

The new improved TVL2000 II™ features a low heat high CRI dynamic LED array panel consisting of 450 warm white and 450 cool white LEDs, variable manual and DMX controlled color temperature (3,200K - 6,500K) and dimming, adjustable barn doors, built-in magnetic gel/diffuser holder, flicker free operation for broadcast TV and FILM, 3 and 5 pin DMX in/out and linkable power in/out connections, (4) button (2) dial LCD display control panel, 90W max power consumption, and a multi-voltage universal auto switching power supply (100-240v).
 Low Heat High CRI Dynamic LED Array Panel
 Variable Color Temperature Settings (3,200K - 6,500K)
 Adjustable Barn Doors
 Built-In Magnetic Gel/Diffuser Holder
 Manual and DMX Controlled Color Temp and Dimming
 Flicker Free Operation for Broadcast TV and FILM
 Multiple Unit Power Linking
