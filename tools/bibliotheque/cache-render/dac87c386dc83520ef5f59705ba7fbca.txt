Kiloview N60 is a 4K HDMI all-in-one bi-directional converter, delivering next-level IP video performance. With AVC, HEVC, and NDI codecs, it supports NDI High Bandwidth, NDI HX2/HX3, SRT, RTMP, HLS, TS-over-UDP, RTP, RTSP and WHIP.

Built for professional broadcast, it delivers exceptional 4:2:2 10-bit HDR image quality. For remote and cross-network workflows, the N60 also supports seamless internet transmission through the NDI Bridge Utility. Additional features such as PoE, Tally, PTZ control, KIS Pro intercom, and 3.5mm audio embedding further complete its professional IP production capabilities.

Fully compatible with the latest NDI 6.3, the N60 delivers enhanced device monitoring, remote receiver management, and seamless interoperability for modern IP production workflows.

Bi-directional Encoding & Decoding Converter

The N60 is a versatile converter that integrates both encoding and decoding functionalities in one compact unit.

In encoding mode, the N60 easily converts a 4K HDMI video into NDI High Bandwidth, NDI HX2/HX3, SRT or RTMP video streams.
In decoding mode, it automatically detects and adapts to incoming NDI High Bandwidth, NDI HX2/HX3, SRT, or RTMP streams. A loop-out also allows monitoring on a display or projector for easy video signal check in real time.

With its dual-mode flexibility, the N60 is ideal for a wide range of professional AV workflows.

4K HDR Tier-1 Image Quality

The N60 delivers breathtaking visual clarity with 4K HDR support and 4:2:2 10-bit color precision—every frame is rich in detail, smooth in tone, and true to life, making it ideal for demanding professional productions.

Comprehensive Protocol Support for Seamless Format Conversion

The N60 supports all major IP transmission protocols—NDI High Bandwidth, NDI HX2, NDI HX3, SRT, RTMP, RTSP, HLS, UDP, RTP and WHIP, allowing effortless conversion and interoperability between different streaming formats.
Whether you’re pushing a high-quality NDI feed to the network or pulling an SRT or RTMP stream for local output, the N60 bridges protocols and devices, ensuring smooth operation in any hybrid workflow.

Broadcast-Grade Interlaced
and Progressive Signal Handling

The N60 fully supports 1080i50/i60 interlaced signals throughout the entire processing chain:
• 1080i50/i60 in → 1080i50/i60 out
• 1080i50/i60 in → 1080p50/p60 out
This flexibility ensures smooth operation in studios working with both interlaced and progressive formats.

It also supports SEI timecode insertion for NDI HX and NetStream, enabling synchronization of multi-camera feeds using either system time or source device time.

*The N60 processes 1080i50/i60 signals and provides native 1080i50/i60 output. This feature is available when the device utilizes ​ NDI HB or H.265 codec, but is ​not supported under the H.264 codec.

Flexible Audio Options,
Just the Way You Need

The N60 offers comprehensive audio integration to suit a wide range of production scenarios:
• HDMI audio embedding & de-embedding for direct mixer connection
• Real-time analog audio embedding via 3.5mm input
• Up to 8-channel audio encoding
• Up to 16-channel audio decoding with flexible 16-to-8 channel mapping.

Large-sized Color
LCD Screen with Tally

• Clear Status Display with Intuitive Navigation: real-time display of network status, operating mode, input signal information, and device status keeps you fully informed, while the intuitive scroll wheel allows quick navigation and precise adjustment of key settings.
• Tally Indicators: Front panel LCD, rear Tally LEDs, and external Tally support provide clear red/green indicators for PGM (program) and PVW (preview) status.

Redundant Power Supply wtih PoE

The N60 features dual Gigabit Ethernet ports, one supporting PoE for combined power and network over a single cable.
The dual network port design provides primary-backup redundancy. If the primary port fails, the backup port ensures service continuity. Additionally, these ports support bonding function, allowing different network to share data transmission—ensuring uninterrupted video streaming.​
