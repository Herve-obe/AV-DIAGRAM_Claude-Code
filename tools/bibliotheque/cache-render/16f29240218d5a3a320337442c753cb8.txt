Step up to the mic that built the sound of broadcast. The RE20's continuously Variable-D design holds your low end steady on and off axis - no bass-heavy proximity bump, no thin edge when you back away. A large Acoustalloy diaphragm handles huge SPLs without flinching, an internal pop filter tames plosives, and a hum-bucking coil shrugs off magnetic noise. Radio booth, podcast desk, kick drum, bass cab - wherever a true voice has to land, it lands.

Key features
Move freely on mic
Continuously Variable-D holds tone consistent off-axis, no proximity boom.
Plosives handled
Integral blast and wind filter tames hard consonants without an add-on screen.
Clean signal near the gear
Hum-bucking coil and steel case reject magnetically induced hum.
Take the loud stuff
Large Acoustalloy diaphragm reproduces high SPLs without overload.
One mic, many rooms
Broadcast, podcast, voiceover, kick drum, and bass cab, with a bass-tilt switch on tap.
Plug in and go
Delivers studio condenser response with no powering required.
